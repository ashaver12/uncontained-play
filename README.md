# UNCONTAINED — Playtest 2

**[Play in your browser](https://ashaver12.github.io/uncontained-play/)** — computer, iPhone or Android. Nothing to install. On a phone, turn it sideways.

**[Android app (APK)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ashaver12/uncontained-play/apk/UNCONTAINED-2.apk)** — optional, for Android phones.

You are an invasive species: fungus, vines, an insect swarm or feral animals. You are not trying to wipe the world out. You are trying to take hold: grow living territory, keep it alive while people try to clear it, and hold your ground.

1. Open the link and tap **First expedition · guided**. One run takes about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. When the run ends, tap **Give feedback**. Three taps and one optional line. It opens your own email with everything filled in.

This repository holds only the built playtest. It is replaced automatically whenever a new build is published.
